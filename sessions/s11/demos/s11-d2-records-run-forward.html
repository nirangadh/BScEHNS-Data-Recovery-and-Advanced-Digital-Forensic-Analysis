WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The records captured only what happened after they were switched on. The upload and the read before 10:05 left no record anywhere: Event history keeps only management events, and the trail and the server access logs were off. Asking the next day changed nothing, because no record can be written afterwards for the past. That is section 5's rule, and it is why your lab switches every record on before the incident, and why the coursework begins with readiness.
