WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Every overwrite added a version and the delete only added a delete marker, so the current view looked empty while all three versions stayed in the bucket, each with its version ID. Without versioning, the same actions left nothing. This is section 7's cloud form of Session 2's idea that deletion removes the pointer, with one difference: here the history survives by design, until someone with permission deletes a version itself. Your lab switches versioning on before the incident so that this history exists for Session 12.
