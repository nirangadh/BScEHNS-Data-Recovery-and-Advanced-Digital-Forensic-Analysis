      WHAT YOU JUST SAW

      The responsibility line moved up the stack at each step: IaaS gave the provider the hardware and the virtualisation, PaaS also the operating system, SaaS also the application. The evidence you can collect yourself moved with it. Amazon S3 behaves like PaaS for evidence: you reach the objects, their metadata and only the records you switched on. That is section 3's rule, more control lower down, less the higher you go, and it is why readiness in section 5 is the customer's job.
