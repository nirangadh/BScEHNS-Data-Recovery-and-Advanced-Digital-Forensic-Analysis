WHAT YOU JUST SAW

You counted how often the byte values appear in four files. Plain text used a few values very often and most not at all, so its entropy was low. The JPEG, the container and the wiped letter all used every value almost equally, so their entropy was close to 8.

High entropy alone therefore proves little. The JPEG had a file signature and the other two had none. Data with high entropy and no signature or structure is consistent with encryption, and it is equally consistent with wiping. In a report you state the measurement, and you look for the program that would explain it.
