WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The same letter ended in two ways. When it was only deleted, the record was marked as not in use and the clusters kept their content, so a tool followed the record and recovered the letter exactly.

When it was wiped first, the content was overwritten before the record was released. The record survived in both cases. After wiping it still gives a size and a list of clusters, but under a random name, and the clusters hold nothing that a person wrote. That record is your evidence: it shows that a file of this size existed, and its name and content are the mark of a wiping tool.
