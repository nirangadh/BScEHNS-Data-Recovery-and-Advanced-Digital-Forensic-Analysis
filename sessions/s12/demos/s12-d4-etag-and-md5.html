WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The same content gave three different ETags. Only the single-part upload with SSE-S3 had an ETag equal to the MD5 value of the content; the multipart ETag was built from the parts' MD5 values and ended in -3, and the SSE-KMS ETag was unrelated. A SHA-256 value of a downloaded copy matched in every case. This is section 6: your incident files were small single-part SSE-S3 uploads, so in the lab each ETag matches one local file, and where an ETag cannot be compared you compare downloaded copies instead.
