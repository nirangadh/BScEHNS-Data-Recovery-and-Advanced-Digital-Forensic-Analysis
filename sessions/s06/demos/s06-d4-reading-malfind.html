S06-D4: Reading a malfind finding

What this shows: a region is reported because of two properties, its protection and the missing file behind it, and neither property says who put the content there or why.

A made-up machine. The names of the programs are not from the case.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The plugin applied two tests: writable and executable at once, and private with no file behind it. Two regions passed. One belonged to a program that creates such memory in normal work. The other began with the header of a Windows program, and the plugin could not say how it got there. In a case, a line from malfind is a lead: the finding comes from the parent, the command line, the modules, the connections and the content of the region, and a report states what was seen before it states what it means.
