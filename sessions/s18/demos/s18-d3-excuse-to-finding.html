WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Each phrase of the excuse failed for its own reason: a judgement with no observation, a fact about the writer, and an effect with no result named. The rebuilt limitation has the three parts of Session 13's method: what was seen, with its time; its effect on one conclusion; and the files that show it.

For your report: the gap was real in both versions. Only the second tells a reader how far to rely on each finding. The account and its values are the invented ones from Session 13; yours come from your own files. Back to section 3 of the notes.
