WHAT YOU JUST SAW

One finding moved from the lower band to the upper band, and each move came from one element that the descriptors ask for: named evidence, times in UTC, their source clocks, and a gap with its effect. The confident sentence doubled the length and moved nothing, because no descriptor rewards length or certainty.

For your report: read each descriptor as a list of elements, find each element in your draft, and write the missing one as your next change. The criterion here is a practice one, not your coursework's; use your own summary rubric from the LMS. Back to section 1 of the notes.
