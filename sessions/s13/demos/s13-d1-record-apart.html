WHAT YOU JUST SAW

    
One event answered six questions, each from its own field: who from userIdentity, what from eventName and requestParameters, when from eventTime, from where from sourceIPAddress, with which program from userAgent, and which request from requestID. A management event fills the same fields differently. This is Section 1 of the notes; in the lab, your jq filter chooses exactly these fields from every event.
