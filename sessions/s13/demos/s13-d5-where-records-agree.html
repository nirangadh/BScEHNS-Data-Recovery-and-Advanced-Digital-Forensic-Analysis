S13-D5: Where the records agree

What this shows: a timeline built from three sources holds firm where they agree and shows a stated gap wherever one source is silent.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Each event was tested against three records, two written by AWS and one by you. Where all three agreed, the timeline was firm; where one was silent, the silence became a stated gap with a reason, not a missing row. The last event showed the hard case: without your own log, the two provider records had to test each other. This is Section 8 of the notes and its pivotal idea: a timeline is an argument built from records you did not write; it holds only where they agree, and its gaps must be stated.
