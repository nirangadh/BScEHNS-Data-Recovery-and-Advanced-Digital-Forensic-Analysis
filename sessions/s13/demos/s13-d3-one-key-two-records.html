WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The request ID worked as a join key, exactly as the Community ID did in Session 8: one shared value across two records. Where it was missing, time, key, address and the kind of request made a weaker join, marked as such. Every row left alone was kept and given a reason from the evidence: a line not yet delivered, a request outside what one record covers, a failure that only one record holds. This is Section 4 and Section 5 of the notes; in the lab, filling the reason column is your work.
