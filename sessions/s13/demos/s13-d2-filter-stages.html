S13-D2: A filter, one stage at a time

What this shows: each stage of a jq filter either removes events or changes their shape, so the rows you get are decided stage by stage.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Each stage of the filter did one job: open the list, keep S3, keep the case bucket, choose fields, print rows, and then the sort put them in time order. Two stages removed events; one changed their shape; the sort was needed because CloudTrail stores events in no guaranteed order. This is Section 2 of the notes. In the lab you build the same filter, and checking each stage's output is how you find a mistake before it reaches your timeline.
