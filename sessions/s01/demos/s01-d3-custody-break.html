S01-D3: A gap in the custody record

What this shows: a gap at one handover puts every later finding in doubt, while the earlier entries and findings still stand.

What the unit wants to tell the court

Your turn. Remove one entry and see what it puts in doubt.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

A custody record works in one direction. A missing entry does not damage what came before it, but it puts everything after it in doubt, because every later action depends on an item whose history has a hole. The earlier the gap, the more is lost.

You also saw the limit of the hash value. It can show that the content did not change. It cannot show who held the item. The unit needs both, and it can only have both if each entry is written at the moment of the action. A gap cannot be repaired later.
