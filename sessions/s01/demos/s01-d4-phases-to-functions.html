S01-D4: Four phases become six Functions

What this shows: the same incident response activities regroup from the four phases of Rev 2 into the six Functions of Rev 3, and learning moves from the end to everywhere.

2Detection and Analysis

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Every Rev 2 phase found a place among the Rev 3 Functions, so the activities of incident response did not change. What changed is the grouping. Preparation became three Functions that run all the time, the response became Detect, Respond and Recover, and learning stopped being a final phase.

For the investigation this means two things. Your forensic work sits mainly in Respond, where incident data is collected and its integrity is protected. And when you name the NIST lifecycle in a report or an answer, you must say which revision: four phases are Rev 2, six Functions are Rev 3.
