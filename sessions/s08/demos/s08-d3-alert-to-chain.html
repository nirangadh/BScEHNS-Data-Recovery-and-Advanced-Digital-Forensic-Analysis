S08-D3: From one alert to the chain

What this shows: an alert and a flow record of the same connection carry the same Community ID, and that ID joins one alert to the flows before and after it, which show the chain of the attack.

The alert

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

One alert became a chain of three steps, because you joined two records of the same capture. The Community ID is the join key: both tools calculate it from the same five values, so the same connection gets the same ID. The join also found flows that raised no alert at all. Correlation turns a single alert into a story that you can check step by step.
