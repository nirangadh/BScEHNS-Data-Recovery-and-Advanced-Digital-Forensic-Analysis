    WHAT YOU JUST SAW

    Signature-based detection finds what is already known, and it is blind to anything new. Anomaly-based detection finds the unusual, and it is blind to an attack that looks normal. Every threshold trades false positives for false negatives, so no setting removes both. This is why an alert is only a hypothesis: a person must check it against the traffic before it becomes a finding.
