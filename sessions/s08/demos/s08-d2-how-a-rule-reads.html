        WHAT YOU JUST SAW

        A rule is a list of conditions, and an alert needs every condition to match. So reading a rule tells you exactly what an alert means and what it does not mean. The message is a label that the author of the rule wrote, not a fact about the traffic. Before you report an alert, open the packets that fired it.
