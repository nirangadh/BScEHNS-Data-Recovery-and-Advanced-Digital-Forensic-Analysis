S08-D5: How long an indicator stays useful

What this shows: an indicator of compromise is useful only until the attacker changes the thing it describes, and some things are much cheaper to change than others.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Every indicator has a useful life. The indicators that are easiest to collect, such as hash values and addresses, are the ones the attacker changes first. A good list mixes exact indicators for certainty with behaviour for staying power, and it says where each indicator came from and when. An indicator from an unconfirmed alert does not belong on the list.
