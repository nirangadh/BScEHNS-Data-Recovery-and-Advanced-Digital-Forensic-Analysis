S08-D4: Two timelines on one clock

What this shows: when you put two records on one clock, merge them and sort them, the order of the rows shows whether they can be about the same events.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Putting two records on one clock and sorting them is a test, not only a way to tidy rows. When the rows of the two records interleave within seconds, the records support each other. When they stay in two blocks, years apart, the comparison excludes a link. So correlation can exclude as well as confirm, and both results belong in your report, each with its limits.
