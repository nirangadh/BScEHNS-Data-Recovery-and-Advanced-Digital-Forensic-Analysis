S05-D3: Two clocks in one timeline

What this shows: a source that records local time puts its event hours out of place, until every source is normalised to UTC.

TimeClockSourceEvent
SYSTEM\ControlSet001\Control\TimeZoneInformation: ActiveTimeBias = 240 minutes. UTC = local time + 4 hours.

Four real records from the morning of 24 March 2015 in the case.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Four honest records gave an impossible order, because three stored UTC and one stored local time. Reading the offset from the image and converting the one local source put the stick's connection where it belongs, two minutes after the last rename. Converting every source, including those already in UTC, broke the order again. This is why every source is normalised to UTC before events are merged, and why a timeline keeps the original time and the clock of each source beside the converted time.
