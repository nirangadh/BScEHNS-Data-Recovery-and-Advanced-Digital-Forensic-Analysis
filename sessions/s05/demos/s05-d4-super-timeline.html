WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Merging and sorting gave what no single artefact could: the order of events across sources. It also gave 146,560 rows, and the events that mattered were five of them. Filtering made the list readable, and one careless filter removed the most important row without any warning. This is what a super-timeline adds and what it buries: use it to find the order, record every filter, and confirm each row that you rely on in the artefact behind it.
