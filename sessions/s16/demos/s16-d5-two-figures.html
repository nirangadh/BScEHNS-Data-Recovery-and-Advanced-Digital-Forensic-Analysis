S16-D5: One finding, two figures

What this shows: a clean figure can hide a gap; the honest one shows the gap and its cause.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The same data was drawn twice. The smooth line was clean and easy to read, and it hid the one fact that changes the reading: a day whose records were deleted and cannot be recovered. The honest figure kept the clean style, left the day empty, drew the gap with its cause, and gave a caption with the source, the tool and its version, the clock and the limitation.

For the investigation: a finding no one understands is not a finding, and a figure that hides its gap is not honest. Make the limitation as legible as the pattern. Back to section 8 of the notes.
