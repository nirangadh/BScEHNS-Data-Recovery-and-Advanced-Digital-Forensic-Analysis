WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Deleting a row removed its pointer from the page's list, not its bytes. A cell among live cells became a freeblock, losing only its first 4 bytes to the freeblock's note; the cell nearest the free space was left whole inside it. A recovery tool reads those bytes, but a record whose start was overwritten is a fragment until its fields are checked. With secure delete, as on Android, the same deletion leaves zeros, and the header gives no sign of it.

For the investigation: open the free space and look before you say whether deleted rows survive. Zeros in every freeblock are evidence in themselves, and belong in your report as a limitation. Back to section 2 of the notes.
