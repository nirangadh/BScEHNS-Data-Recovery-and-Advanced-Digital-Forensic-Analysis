Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

A correct findings paragraph became an executive summary by moving the tool, path, tables and clock units out, keeping what happened in plain words, keeping the gap, stating how sure the writer is and why, and ending with what should follow. Nothing in the summary is new: every sentence rests on a finding in the paragraph.

For the investigation: write the summary last, for a reader who reads nothing else, and test each sentence against a finding. Your lab section's summary is about different findings; this shows the method, not your text. Back to section 7 of the notes.
