S16-D3: Three ways to open one database

What this shows: on the test phone's own files, how a database is opened decides what survives.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

On the test phone's own files, an ordinary open checkpointed the write-ahead log into the database and deleted it, so the newest data looked fine while every older version was lost. A read-only open kept the database and the log, and changed only the shm file, which holds no evidence of its own. An open that ignored the log showed no tables, because this app's whole database lives in its log.

For the investigation: hash before you open, open only copies, open them read-only, and copy every companion file with its name unchanged. Write any change you see, with its cause. Back to section 4 of the notes.
