S14-D5: The attribution ladder

What this shows: each step from a message to a person needs its own evidence, and when one source is missing, the step it supported loses its support.

FROM THE MESSAGE (BOTTOM) TO A PERSON (TOP)

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

You climbed from a message to a person one rung at a time, and every rung lit only when its own evidence arrived: the store, the provider's sign-in records, the internet provider's record, the laptop, the logon records, and finally evidence from outside every computer. When a source was missing, its rung went dark and the report had to say so; without evidence at the top, the sentence about a person could not be written.

This is section 8 of the notes and the pivotal idea of the session: online identity is a mask, and attribution links the mask to a person step by step. In your attribution note, each step names its evidence, and the step where the evidence ends is stated, not skipped.
