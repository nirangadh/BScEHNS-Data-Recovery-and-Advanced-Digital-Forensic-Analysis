What this shows: some header lines are written by the sender's program and can say anything, others are added by receiving servers, so a forged From line is caught by the lines the sender could not write.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

You sorted every header line by its author. The sender's program wrote From, To, Subject, Date, Message-ID and Reply-To, and could have made any of them say anything. The receiving server added Return-Path, the Received line and the results of SPF, DKIM and DMARC. In the forged message the sender's lines told one story, and the server's lines contradicted it.

This is section 4 of the notes. In a report, name the line each fact comes from and who wrote it: a fact from a line the sender wrote is the sender's claim.
