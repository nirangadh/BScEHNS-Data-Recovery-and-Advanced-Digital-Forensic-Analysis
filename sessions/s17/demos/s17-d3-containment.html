S17-D3: Containment that keeps the evidence

What this shows: the order of the first actions on a compromised server decides which evidence survives.

    WHAT THE INVESTIGATION STILL HAS

    Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

    WHAT YOU JUST SAW

    Both orders stopped the theft. Switching off first lost the processes, the connections and the memory at once, and restoring first lost the disk and its logs as well. Isolating the running host stopped the harm with the power still on, so each kind of evidence could be captured in the order of volatility and copied off before anything was cleaned or restored.

    For the investigation: the first ten minutes decide what the case can ever prove. Agree the order with the client before the day it is needed, in its playbook. Back to section 4 of the notes.
