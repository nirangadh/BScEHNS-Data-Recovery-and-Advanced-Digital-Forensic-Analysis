What this shows: each broken step between collection and court gives the defence a specific challenge to the method.

Keyboard: the left and right arrow keys move between steps.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The exhibit reached court through seven steps, each with its own record. A gap in the custody record, an undisclosed disagreement between tools, an unconverted clock and a limitation removed at the client's request each gave the defence a specific challenge, aimed at the method rather than at the conclusion.

For the investigation: a finding is only as usable as its weakest step. Keep the record of every step, and keep your duty to the court above your client. Back to section 6 of the notes.
