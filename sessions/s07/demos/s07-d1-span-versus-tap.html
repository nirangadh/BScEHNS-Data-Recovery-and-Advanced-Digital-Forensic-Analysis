WHAT YOU JUST SAW

The mirror port (SPAN) had to fit 1200 Mbit/s of copies into one 1000 Mbit/s port, so the switch dropped 200 Mbit/s with no warning, while the real traffic passed untouched. The TAP gave each direction its own output and kept every frame. This is the idea in the notes: a capture is only as complete as its capture point, so record where and how it was taken. In a case, a missing packet is not proof that nothing was sent.
