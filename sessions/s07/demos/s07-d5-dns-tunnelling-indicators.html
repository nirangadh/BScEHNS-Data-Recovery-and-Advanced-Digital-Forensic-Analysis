WHAT YOU JUST SAW

You set two lists of DNS queries side by side and saw four indicators: long labels that look random, many names that are each asked only once, an unusual record type, and a steady rhythm. As the notes explain, the hidden data must travel inside the names, so the names give the channel away. This matters because an examiner can find the pattern in DNS logs or flow records, even when no content can be read. Report it as a pattern towards one domain, not as proof of what was sent.
