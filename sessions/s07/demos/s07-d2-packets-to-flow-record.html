S07-D2: Packets fold into a flow record

What this shows: a router or probe folds many packets into flow records, which keep who, where, when and how much, and keep none of the content.

WHAT YOU JUST SAW

Eight packets of one conversation were folded into two flow records, one for each direction. A flow record answers who, to where, when, how much and by which protocol. It never answers what was said, because it keeps none of the data the packets carried. This matters because flow records are small enough to keep for months, so they often exist when no capture does.
