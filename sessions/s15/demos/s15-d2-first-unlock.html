WHAT YOU JUST SAW

You moved one phone through its states. Before first unlock, only the keys that need no passcode exist; after the first unlock, nearly all keys stay in memory until the phone restarts, even with the screen locked; a restart or a flat battery throws them away again.

This is section 3 of the notes. The state of the phone at seizure decides what any extraction can hold, so it belongs in the extraction's record. The test phone was extracted after first unlock, which is why its app folders are readable.
