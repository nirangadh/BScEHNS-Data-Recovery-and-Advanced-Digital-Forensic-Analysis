## The two assessments

| Assessment | Weight | What it assesses |
|---|---|---|
| Coursework (CW1): cloud forensics report and viva | 25% | Learning outcomes 1, 2, 4 and 5 |
| Phase test | 75% | Learning outcomes 1, 2, 3 and 4 |

## The coursework

You investigate a suspected insider incident in a cloud storage environment that you build yourself, to a given design. You collect and protect the logs, recover and examine changed and deleted files, build a timeline of who did what and when, and recommend how the organisation could detect and investigate such incidents better. You write it up as a professional report of about 2,500 words, not counting references and appendices.

Sessions 11, 12 and 13 build the coursework step by step, and Session 18 is a workshop for it. Create your cloud account when Session 11 tells you to, not earlier: the free account has a limited life, and it must last until your work is marked.

**The viva.** After you submit, you talk with your lecturer for 7 to 10 minutes about your own work: what you did, and why. It is required, and it has no separate mark. It confirms that the work is yours and that you understand it. Where you cannot explain part of your work, the marks for that part reflect what you could explain.

## The phase test

A two-hour, closed-book paper, taken offline and written in an answer script. It covers data recovery and data protection; host, network and malware analysis; mobile file systems and artefacts; and forensic methodology. The format and instructions for your paper are on the LMS.

Short answers are marked on the relevant points you make, not on length. The end-of-day quizzes on this site use the same style, so they are good practice.

## Your own work

Both assessments test your own understanding. The viva exists to confirm it. The rules on academic integrity, and on what help is allowed, are on the LMS.
